# Bee Budgeting Limited - Bear Budgeting App
## Overview
Our app is aimed to help people make budgeting and day-to-day expense tracking a lot easier and pleasant. 

## Featured Screens
# Home screen
This is where the user will log their income and expenses. It takes them to a screen where they can set the amount, date, and category of the income/expense, as well as having the option to attach a photo and/or notes. The user is also able to create a custom category to fit whatever income or expense they need to log.

# Analytics
This screen shows the user a pie chart of either all their expenses or income for the month. It shows the totals per category at the bottom, and the user is able to select which month they'd like to review at the top of the pie chart.

# View all entries
This screen shows the user all their entries (income and expense). It filters by the dates you specify, and there is a toggle that will further filter by categories, so you can see the total categories spent in a date range.

## Features/functionality
#Photo saving
This app lets you take a photo and store it with your log entry.
#Pie chart
Based on the month's expenses or income, the app will create a pie chart showing you exactly what you spent the most money on or got the most money from. 
#History by date range
When looking at the log entry history, you can specify what date range you want to look at.
#Totals per category
Also in the log entry history, you can view the total per category during that date range.
#Local database
There is a local database that stores user information for logging in and sign ups.
#Custom categories
When logging an entry, the user can create a custom category to suit their needs.

## How to use
# Login/Sign up
When you open the app, you will be on the login screen. If you have an account, log in with your details. If you don't, click on the sign up text at the bottom. This will take you to the sign up screen. Add all required details then click sign up. This will take you back to the log in screen, then you must log in with your username and password.
# Home screen
To get started on the home screen, either click on the yellow plus button to log an expense, or the monthly budget progress bar to set a minimum and maximum goal. If you already have entries, they should show up under the history text. To check on Barry, click the bear icon. To see your analytics, click the graph icon. To go back to the home screen, click on the home icon. 
# Logging an entry
To log either an income or an expense, click on the yellow plus icon. This will take you to the logging screen. Select either 'income' or 'expense' to state which of the two your entry belongs to. Then write your amount in, choose your category, select the date and press save. You have the option to attach a photo, via the camera button, and the option to attach a note, via the notes text box. You can also create a new category when selecting categories. 
# Custom category
When choosing the category, click on the 'Create new category' text. This takes you to a new screen. Select your icon, write in the name at the top, and whether the category belongs to income or expense, then click save. The new category will now be added.
# History
To view all of your log entries, click on 'view all' on the home screen. This screen will let you select a date range to see the entries between them. Click on filter when you are ready to see the results. You can also see the total amount spent per category by clicking the group by category checkbox.
# Analytics
On the analytics screen, you can choose to see a pie chart of the selected month's expenses, or the income for that month. If you want to view the analytics for a different month, click on the date to change it. The totals per category for that month will show up at the bottom of the pie chart.
